Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

How do I send you photos?

Send your photos via Etsy messages after ordering. You can send up to 3 photos per message, and the banner accommodates 12 photos in total.

Are there photo requirements?

Yes! Please make sure the photo is high resolution, not a screenshot, and with nothing obstructing the face. Sunglasses are totally okay!

What order are the photos arranged?

Photos are arranged randomly unless you specify a different order. Just let me know in your message!

My event is in one week — can I still order?

Processing time is 7–10 days. For guaranteed delivery within 7 days, purchase the Rush Order fee and message me with your date. Get the Rush Order fee ↗

Can you customize my banner to fit my theme?

Absolutely! Purchase the Custom Order Fee and you can request specific art styles (tropical, baseball, western, and more!). Get the Custom Order fee ↗

What occasions are face banners perfect for?

Birthdays, bachelorette parties, baby showers, graduations, retirement parties, holiday gatherings — any celebration that deserves a personal touch!

Our Story

Joy is handmade here

🌟 Welcome to Miles of Smiles Banner Company! At Miles of Smiles, we believe every celebration deserves a personal touch and a whole lot of joy.

Founded by Liz Bransky, a proud Jewish, Autistic, Latina woman living with Lupus, a devoted wife, and mother to three amazing autistic children, this shop is built on love, resilience, and the magic of meaningful moments.

What began as a labor of love for our own family’s parties has grown into a vibrant collection of handmade banners designed to turn ordinary moments into cherished memories. I know how important it is to make people feel seen, loved, and celebrated.

Each item is crafted by hand with high-quality materials and a big heart. Whether you’re planning a birthday, baby shower, graduation, or “just because” celebration, I’m here to help you add a little magic.

Thank you for supporting a small, woman-owned business where joy is handmade and every order helps lift up a dream.

With gratitude and sparkle,
Liz Bransky 🎉
